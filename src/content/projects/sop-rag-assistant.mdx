## The problem

Employees at VSky needed fast, accurate answers to procedural questions buried in long SOP documents — instead of searching through PDFs or interrupting someone who already knew the answer. I owned the implementation end to end: the retrieval backend, the grounding logic, and the embeddable widget that put it in front of employees.

## What I built

A retrieval-augmented Q&A system built on FastAPI and Gemini, with PostgreSQL and pgvector handling embedding storage and similarity search:

- **Multi-outcome intent routing** — queries are classified first and routed through different pipeline paths, instead of treating every question the same way
- **Metadata-based document selection** — the retriever narrows to the right SOPs using document metadata before touching the vector index
- **Vector retrieval with LLM reranking** — candidate chunks are rescored by the model before generation, so answers are grounded in the strongest evidence rather than the first-pass matches
- **Grounded generation with citations** — answers cite their source SOP sections, not the model's general knowledge
- **Retrieval-audit logging and per-call token-usage tracking** — every retrieval decision and every token is attributable, which is what made cost and quality tunable after launch
- **An embeddable JavaScript widget** — Shadow-DOM isolation means it works in React, Vue, or vanilla JS, mounted by any team with one script tag; bundled through a CI-ready esbuild pipeline

This version was adopted into VSky's product line — used by actual client deployments, not just internally.

## Why it matters

The gap between "a RAG demo that answers questions" and "a RAG system a company will actually put in front of employees" is almost entirely in the parts that aren't retrieval: routing, grounding, observability, and failure handling. Shipping this taught me where enterprise RAG actually breaks — and I'm now working through that gap a second time with more room to push, in the [Intent-Driven RAG System](/projects/intent-driven-rag).
